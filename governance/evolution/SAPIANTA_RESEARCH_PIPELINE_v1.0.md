------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

This document defines the research pipeline of the
SAPIANTA Autonomous Research System.

The research pipeline connects all research components
into a coherent artifact-driven lifecycle.

The pipeline ensures that:

• research is structured
• artifacts are traceable
• experiments are reproducible
• strategies evolve deterministically


------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYSTEM OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

The SAPIANTA research pipeline forms the backbone of
autonomous system evolution.

Pipeline structure:

Idea Discovery Engine
↓
Research Orchestrator
↓
Research Runtime
↓
ASF Experiment Engine
↓
Knowledge Engine
↓
Strategy Evolution Engine
↓
Artifact Registry
↓
Promotion System
↓
Domain Modules
↓
Decision Spine


------------------------------------------------------------
3. ARTIFACT-DRIVEN RESEARCH
------------------------------------------------------------

All research operations in SAPIANTA are artifact-driven.

Key artifact types:

• idea artifact
• experiment artifact
• knowledge artifact
• strategy artifact
• evaluation artifact

Artifacts provide:

• traceability
• deterministic replay
• governance auditability


------------------------------------------------------------
4. IDEA STAGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Ideas are generated by the Idea Discovery Engine.

Sources include:

• research publications
• domain datasets
• internal artifacts
• human proposals

Each idea is converted into an Idea Artifact.


------------------------------------------------------------
5. EXPERIMENT STAGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Ideas are transformed into experiments.

Experiment generation includes:

• hypothesis definition
• parameter configuration
• dataset selection
• evaluation metrics

Experiments must produce Experiment Artifacts.


------------------------------------------------------------
6. EXPERIMENT EXECUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Experiments are executed via the ASF sandbox.

Execution properties:

• deterministic runtime
• reproducible results
• artifact output


------------------------------------------------------------
7. KNOWLEDGE EXTRACTION
------------------------------------------------------------

The Knowledge Engine analyzes experiment artifacts.

It extracts:

• patterns
• parameter sensitivities
• regime behavior
• model insights

Results are stored as Knowledge Artifacts.


------------------------------------------------------------
8. STRATEGY EVOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

The Strategy Evolution Engine uses knowledge artifacts
to produce improved strategies.

Evolution techniques include:

• parameter mutation
• strategy recombination
• regime specialization

Each result becomes a Strategy Artifact.


------------------------------------------------------------
9. EVALUATION STAGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Strategies are evaluated against defined metrics.

Evaluation criteria include:

• profitability
• stability
• robustness
• reproducibility

Evaluation results are stored as Evaluation Artifacts.


------------------------------------------------------------
10. PROMOTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Strategies that satisfy promotion criteria may be promoted
to operational domains.

Promotion requires:

• artifact lineage
• reproducible experiments
• governance validation


------------------------------------------------------------
11. CONTINUOUS RESEARCH LOOP
------------------------------------------------------------

The research pipeline runs continuously.

Loop structure:

discover → experiment → learn → evolve → evaluate → promote

This enables continuous improvement of the system.


------------------------------------------------------------
12. GOVERNANCE COMPLIANCE
------------------------------------------------------------

All pipeline stages must comply with SAPIANTA governance.

Requirements include:

• artifact registration
• deterministic replay
• traceable lineage
• promotion validation


------------------------------------------------------------
13. SYSTEM ROLE
------------------------------------------------------------

The research pipeline transforms ideas into validated
strategies through deterministic experimentation.

It is the central mechanism through which SAPIANTA
learns and evolves.
