------------------------------------------------------------
1. SYSTEM PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

SAPIANTA is a deterministic autonomous research system.

Its goal is to discover, evaluate, and deploy strategies
through a structured research pipeline.

The system is governance-first and artifact-driven.


------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE PRINCIPLES
------------------------------------------------------------

The system enforces:

• deterministic execution
• artifact lineage
• reproducible experiments
• governance validation


------------------------------------------------------------
3. AUTONOMOUS RESEARCH LOOP
------------------------------------------------------------

discover → experiment → learn → evaluate → evolve → promote


------------------------------------------------------------
4. CORE RESEARCH COMPONENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

Idea Discovery Engine

Collects ideas from:

• research papers
• datasets
• internal artifacts
• human proposals


Research Orchestrator

Coordinates the research system.


Research Runtime

Executes the continuous research loop.


Experiment Engine

Transforms ideas into experiments.


Evaluation Engine

Analyzes experiment results and ranks strategies.


Promotion Engine

Promotes validated strategies into operational domains.


Artifact Registry

Stores all research artifacts and lineage.
