------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

This document defines the high-level architecture of the
SAPIANTA Autonomous Research System.

The system continuously discovers, evaluates,
and evolves strategies through deterministic research loops.

The architecture separates:

• research
• governance
• operational decision execution


------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE COMPONENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

The Autonomous Research System consists of:

Idea Discovery Engine

Collects research ideas from:

• human proposals
• research papers
• datasets
• internal artifacts


Research Orchestrator

Coordinates the research workflow.


Research Runtime

Executes research cycles and experiment pipelines.


Experiment Engine

Runs experiments to evaluate strategy candidates.


Evaluation Engine

Analyzes experiment results and produces
performance metrics.


Strategy Evolution Engine

Generates improved strategies through:

• parameter search
• mutation
• recombination


Promotion Engine

Validates and promotes successful strategies
into operational domains.


Artifact Registry

Stores all artifacts generated by the system.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. SYSTEM LAYERS
------------------------------------------------------------

Layer 0 — Constitution

System invariants and governance rules.


Layer 1 — Artifact System

Artifact storage and lineage tracking.


Layer 2 — Research Execution

Experiment execution and runtime orchestration.


Layer 3 — Evolution System

Strategy evolution and promotion control.


Layer 4 — Domain Systems

Domain implementations:

• trading
• credit
• energy
• logistics


Layer 5 — Decision Spine

Operational decision validation and execution.


------------------------------------------------------------
6. GOVERNANCE ROLE
------------------------------------------------------------

Governance ensures that the autonomous research
system remains:

• deterministic
• reproducible
• auditable
• safe for operational deployment.


------------------------------------------------------------
7. SYSTEM PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

The goal of the SAPIANTA Autonomous Research System
is to create a continuously improving decision system.

The system discovers better strategies through
structured experimentation and controlled evolution.
