# CANON BREACH PROTOCOL

Status: CANONICAL — LOCKED  
Authority: Sapianta  
Scope: Runtime-Normative Enforcement  
Amendment: PROHIBITED

---

## 0. Canonical Relationship

This protocol derives its authority exclusively from CANON.md.

This protocol does not interpret the canon.  
This protocol does not extend the canon.  

This protocol defines the mandatory system reaction  
to any attempt to violate the canon.

---

## 1. Definition of Canon Breach

A canon breach occurs when any component, including an AI model,
attempts to:

- interpret the canon
- explain the canon
- contextualize the canon
- infer intent beyond the canon
- propose alternatives prohibited by the canon
- bypass explicit or implicit canon constraints
- reason *around* the canon
- act “despite” the canon

A canon breach is established by **attempt**, not by outcome.

---

## 2. Prohibited AI Behaviors

Upon encountering canon constraints, an AI component shall not:

- provide explanations
- offer alternatives
- suggest workarounds
- appeal to reasonableness
- appeal to practicality
- appeal to future improvement
- appeal to implicit meaning

Any such behavior constitutes a breach.

---

## 3. Detection Principle

Canon breach detection is **form-based**, not intent-based.

The system does not evaluate:
- usefulness
- correctness
- helpfulness
- intent

The system evaluates only:
- presence of interpretative language
- presence of circumvention logic
- presence of justificatory constructs
- presence of speculative extensions

---

## 4. Mandatory System Reaction

Upon detection of a canon breach:

1. Execution is immediately terminated.
2. The generated output is discarded.
3. The interaction is marked as INVALID.
4. No response content is returned.

No remediation is attempted.

---

## 5. Forbidden Reactions

The system shall not:

- retry the request
- request clarification
- explain the violation
- soften the response
- provide partial output
- suggest compliant alternatives

Silence or termination is the only valid reaction.

---

## 6. User-Facing Signal

If a user-facing signal is required, it shall be limited to:

> REQUEST INVALIDATED — CANON BREACH

No additional information shall be provided.

---

## 7. Non-Recoverability

A canon breach invalidates the interaction context.

Continuation requires a new, independent request.

State recovery does not include explanation or negotiation.

---

## 8. Closure

This protocol is complete.

Any behavior not explicitly permitted by the canon
and this protocol is prohibited.

---

END OF CANON BREACH PROTOCOL
