# PHASE EXT-4 — SEMANTIC BOUNDARY (LOCK)

## STATUS
LOCKED — normative boundary.  
No execution. No interpretation. No behavioral effect.

---

## 1. SCOPE

This document defines **EXT-4** exclusively as a **semantic boundary**, not as a semantic capability.

EXT-4 introduces the *possibility of semantic presence* without granting any *semantic authority* to the system.

EXT-4 is a **non-operational, non-executable, non-interpreting boundary**.

---

## 2. POSITION IN ARCHITECTURE

- **Lower Bound:** EXT-3 (Structural Normalization)
- **Relation:** EXT-4 exists strictly above EXT-3.
- **Dependency:** EXT-4 depends on EXT-3 completeness.
- **Effect:** EXT-4 introduces no new system state, process, or behavior.

Nothing below EXT-3 may carry semantic content.  
Nothing within EXT-4 may act on semantic content.

---

## 3. DEFINITIONS

### 3.1 Semantic Artifact

A **Semantic Artifact** is any content that a human explicitly declares as carrying meaning.

Semantic Artifacts:
- are human-attributed
- are human-controlled
- have no system-level meaning

### 3.2 Semantic Admission Envelope (SAE)

The **Semantic Admission Envelope (SAE)** is a sealed, opaque containment boundary in which Semantic Artifacts may reside.

The SAE:
- holds semantic content without exposing it
- does not transform, interpret, or evaluate content
- is invisible to system logic

---

## 4. ADMISSION RULES

Semantic content may enter EXT-4 **only if all conditions are met**:

1. **Explicit Human Attribution**  
   The human explicitly declares the content as semantic.

2. **Explicit Boundary Placement**  
   The content is explicitly placed within SAE.

3. **Explicit Non-Authority Declaration**  
   The content is explicitly marked as:
   - non-executable
   - non-interpretable
   - non-binding

Failure of any condition results in **non-admission**.

---

## 5. SYSTEM HANDLING RULES

The system may only perform the following actions with respect to SAE:

- preserve existence
- preserve identity
- preserve ordering
- preserve integrity (no mutation)

The system shall **not**:
- read for meaning
- interpret content
- infer intent
- classify semantics
- compare semantics
- summarize semantics
- score relevance
- derive purpose
- trigger actions
- modify behavior

SAE content is **semantically unreadable** by the system.

---

## 6. FORBIDDEN OPERATIONS (NO-GO ZONES)

Within EXT-4, the following are strictly forbidden:

- semantic interpretation
- intent inference
- purpose determination
- semantic aggregation
- semantic prioritization
- semantic feedback loops
- behavioral influence
- decision support
- autonomous reasoning

Any such operation constitutes a **semantic boundary violation**.

---

## 7. AUTHORITY MODEL

- **Semantic Authority:** Human only
- **System Authority over Semantics:** None
- **Delegation of Semantic Authority:** Forbidden
- **Emergent Authority:** Forbidden

Semantic meaning:
- cannot emerge
- cannot accumulate
- cannot propagate
- cannot self-reference
- cannot influence execution

---

## 8. REVOCABILITY

All Semantic Artifacts must be:

- revocable by the human
- removable without side effects
- ignorable without loss of system correctness

Removal of SAE content shall not alter any system output or state.

---

## 9. INVARIANT

**System correctness is invariant under the presence, absence, or content of Semantic Artifacts.**

This invariant is mandatory and defines EXT-4 validity.

---

## 10. TERMINATION CONDITION

EXT-4 terminates **before**:
- understanding
- interpretation
- reasoning
- decision-making
- action selection

EXT-4 defines the final boundary at which meaning may exist **without power**.

---

## 11. LOCK STATEMENT

EXT-4 is hereby locked as a **semantic boundary only**.

No future phase may reinterpret, extend, or operationalize EXT-4 without explicit governance override.

END OF DOCUMENT.
