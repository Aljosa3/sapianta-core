## PHASE 11 — EXPLICIT PROHIBITIONS

### P11-1 — No Execution
FAZA 11 MUST NOT execute, simulate, or trigger any behavior,
logic, process, or action.

### P11-2 — No Permission Granting
FAZA 11 MUST NOT grant, imply, or encode permission to execute.

### P11-3 — No Authorization Decisions
FAZA 11 MUST NOT evaluate, resolve, or decide authorization.

### P11-4 — No Signals or Flags
FAZA 11 MUST NOT emit signals, flags, states, or readiness indicators.

### P11-5 — No Core Interaction
FAZA 11 MUST NOT invoke, reference, or interact with Core,
Handshake, Boundary Adapter, Governance Gate, or System Flow.

### P11-6 — No Canon Interaction
FAZA 11 MUST NOT reference, interpret, explain, or depend on Canon content.

### P11-7 — No Behavioral Impact
FAZA 11 MUST NOT alter or influence system behavior,
flow, routing, or governance outcomes.

### P11-8 — No Statefulness
FAZA 11 MUST NOT maintain, infer, or depend on state,
history, counters, or memory.

### P11-9 — No Persistence
FAZA 11 MUST NOT persist, cache, log, replay,
or store authorization-related data.

### P11-10 — No Conditional Logic
FAZA 11 MUST NOT introduce conditions, branching,
comparisons, thresholds, or control flow.

### P11-11 — No Authority Assignment
FAZA 11 MUST NOT assign authority, ownership,
roles, or responsibility for execution.

### P11-12 — No Visibility Expansion
FAZA 11 MUST NOT expose or leak information about
execution capability, readiness, or system internals.

### P11-13 — No Extension Surface
FAZA 11 MUST NOT introduce hooks, plugins,
configuration switches, or dynamic rules.

### P11-14 — No Testing or Simulation
FAZA 11 MUST NOT include tests, mocks, stubs,
emulators, or simulators.

### P11-15 — No Implicit Guarantees
FAZA 11 MUST NOT imply availability, safety,
correctness, or future execution success.

### Binding Clause
Any violation of the above constitutes a Phase 11 breach
and triggers Canon Breach Protocol procedures.
