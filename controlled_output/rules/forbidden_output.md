## CONTROLLED OUTPUT — FORBIDDEN FORMS

### Purpose
Explicitly enumerate forms of output that are prohibited
to prevent implicit execution, intent formation, or authorization.

### Forbidden Forms
A controlled output declaration MUST NOT:
- Contain commands, instructions, or imperative language
- Include executable code, scripts, or pseudo-code
- Encode parameters, payloads, or execution-ready data
- Reference execution gates, authorization, or permissions
- Reference Core, Handshake, Adapter, Governance Gate, or System Flow
- Embed implicit readiness, consent, or clearance signals
- Introduce temporal sequencing or conditional triggers

### Enforcement Note
- No enforcement mechanism is defined in Phase 15
- Presence of forbidden forms constitutes a Phase 15 breach

### Stability
- Forbidden forms are immutable within Phase 15
