## PHASE 10 — EXPLICIT PROHIBITIONS

### P10-1 — No Execution
FAZA 10 MUST NOT execute, simulate, or trigger any logic, process, or behavior.

### P10-2 — No Contract Creation
FAZA 10 MUST NOT introduce new message types, interfaces, or contracts.

### P10-3 — No Contract Modification
FAZA 10 MUST NOT modify, reinterpret, extend, or replace contracts
defined in Phases 07, 08, or 09.

### P10-4 — No Canon Interaction
FAZA 10 MUST NOT reference, interpret, explain, or depend on Canon content.

### P10-5 — No Semantic Interpretation
FAZA 10 MUST NOT infer meaning, intent, correctness, or outcome
from any declared flow step.

### P10-6 — No Behavioral Conditioning
FAZA 10 MUST NOT condition any upstream or downstream behavior
on flow declarations.

### P10-7 — No Statefulness
FAZA 10 MUST NOT maintain, infer, or depend on any state,
history, counters, or memory.

### P10-8 — No Persistence
FAZA 10 MUST NOT persist, cache, replay, or log any flow-related data.

### P10-9 — No Control Flow
FAZA 10 MUST NOT introduce branching, retries, fallbacks,
loops, or timing-based logic.

### P10-10 — No Visibility Expansion
FAZA 10 MUST NOT expose or leak information about Core,
Handshake, Adapter, Governance Gate, or internal decisions.

### P10-11 — No Execution Guarantees
FAZA 10 MUST NOT imply execution, processing,
acceptance, availability, or correctness.

### P10-12 — No Extension Surface
FAZA 10 MUST NOT introduce hooks, plugins, configuration switches,
or dynamic flow rules.

### P10-13 — No Cross-Phase Coupling
FAZA 10 MUST NOT alter, depend on, or influence the behavior,
contracts, or guarantees of other phases.

### P10-14 — No Testing or Simulation
FAZA 10 MUST NOT include tests, mocks, stubs,
emulators, or simulators.

### P10-15 — No Implicit Paths
FAZA 10 MUST NOT imply undocumented, inferred,
or alternative system flows.

### Binding Clause
Any violation of the above constitutes a Phase 10 breach
and triggers Canon Breach Protocol procedures.
