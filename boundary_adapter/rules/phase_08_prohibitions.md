## PHASE 08 — EXPLICIT PROHIBITIONS

### P8-1 — No Execution
FAZA 8 MUST NOT execute any code, logic, or process.

### P8-2 — No Core Invocation
FAZA 8 MUST NOT invoke, call, trigger, or simulate Core or Handshake processing.

### P8-3 — No Handshake Mutation
FAZA 8 MUST NOT modify, extend, reinterpret, or replace Phase 07 handshake contracts.

### P8-4 — No Canon Interaction
FAZA 8 MUST NOT reference, interpret, explain, or depend on Canon content.

### P8-5 — No Semantic Inference
FAZA 8 MUST NOT infer meaning from Interaction inputs or Handshake outputs.

### P8-6 — No Behavioral Conditioning
FAZA 8 MUST NOT condition any behavior on values, states, or responses.

### P8-7 — No Statefulness
FAZA 8 MUST NOT maintain, infer, or depend on state (Core, Interaction, Adapter).

### P8-8 — No Persistence
FAZA 8 MUST NOT persist, cache, replay, or log any data.

### P8-9 — No Control Flow
FAZA 8 MUST NOT implement branching, retries, fallbacks, sequencing, or timing logic.

### P8-10 — No Visibility Expansion
FAZA 8 MUST NOT expose or leak Core or Handshake details beyond declared interfaces.

### P8-11 — No Extension Surface
FAZA 8 MUST NOT introduce hooks, plugins, overrides, or configuration switches.

### P8-12 — No Testing or Simulation
FAZA 8 MUST NOT include tests, mocks, stubs, emulation, or simulators.

### P8-13 — No Cross-Phase Coupling
FAZA 8 MUST NOT create dependencies that alter behavior of other phases.

### P8-14 — No Implicit Guarantees
FAZA 8 MUST NOT imply availability, correctness, safety, or future capability.

### Binding Clause
Any violation of the above constitutes a Phase 08 breach and triggers
Canon Breach Protocol procedures.
