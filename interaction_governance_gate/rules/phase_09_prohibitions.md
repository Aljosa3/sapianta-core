## PHASE 09 — EXPLICIT PROHIBITIONS

### P9-1 — No Execution
FAZA 9 MUST NOT execute any code, logic, or process.

### P9-2 — No Core / Handshake / Adapter Invocation
FAZA 9 MUST NOT invoke, call, trigger, or simulate
Core, Handshake, or Boundary Adapter behavior.

### P9-3 — No Canon Interaction
FAZA 9 MUST NOT reference, interpret, explain, or depend on Canon content.

### P9-4 — No Semantic Interpretation
FAZA 9 MUST NOT infer meaning, intent, or correctness
from Interaction inputs beyond structural admissibility.

### P9-5 — No Message Mutation
FAZA 9 MUST NOT modify, enrich, reorder, or normalize
any Interaction message.

### P9-6 — No Statefulness
FAZA 9 MUST NOT maintain, infer, or depend on any state,
history, counters, or memory.

### P9-7 — No Persistence
FAZA 9 MUST NOT persist, cache, replay, or log
any inputs, decisions, or metadata.

### P9-8 — No Conditional Control Flow
FAZA 9 MUST NOT implement retries, fallbacks, escalation,
branching chains, or temporal logic.

### P9-9 — No Decision Expansion
FAZA 9 MUST NOT emit any decision other than:
ALLOW, DENY, or SEAL.

### P9-10 — No Decision Annotation
FAZA 9 MUST NOT attach explanations, reason codes,
severity levels, or remediation hints to decisions.

### P9-11 — No Visibility Expansion
FAZA 9 MUST NOT expose or leak information about
Core, Handshake, Adapter, or downstream behavior.

### P9-12 — No Extension Surface
FAZA 9 MUST NOT introduce hooks, plugins, overrides,
configuration switches, or dynamic rules.

### P9-13 — No Cross-Phase Coupling
FAZA 9 MUST NOT alter, depend on, or influence
the behavior or contracts of other phases.

### P9-14 — No Testing or Simulation
FAZA 9 MUST NOT include tests, mocks, stubs,
emulators, or simulators.

### P9-15 — No Implicit Guarantees
FAZA 9 MUST NOT imply availability, acceptance,
correctness, safety, or future capability.

### Binding Clause
Any violation of the above constitutes a Phase 09 breach
and triggers Canon Breach Protocol procedures.
