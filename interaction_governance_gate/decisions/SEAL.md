## GOVERNANCE DECISION: SEAL

### Definition
SEAL is a declarative outcome indicating that the input message
is structurally valid but not admissible for progression.

### Effect
- The message is blocked at the Governance Gate.
- The message state is considered closed for progression.

### Guarantees
- No modification
- No forwarding
- No interpretation

### Forbidden Implications
- No partial acceptance
- No conditional release
- No future re-evaluation
