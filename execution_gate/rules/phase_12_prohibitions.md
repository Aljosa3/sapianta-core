## PHASE 12 — EXPLICIT PROHIBITIONS

### P12-1 — No Execution
FAZA 12 MUST NOT execute, simulate, or trigger any execution behavior.

### P12-2 — No Authorization Granting
FAZA 12 MUST NOT grant, imply, or encode authorization or permission to execute.

### P12-3 — No Decision Logic
FAZA 12 MUST NOT evaluate inputs, conditions, or context to produce decisions.

### P12-4 — No Input Consumption
FAZA 12 MUST NOT accept, parse, validate, or consume any inputs.

### P12-5 — No Output Emission
FAZA 12 MUST NOT emit signals, flags, states, decisions, or readiness indicators.

### P12-6 — No Core Interaction
FAZA 12 MUST NOT invoke, reference, or interact with Core in any form.

### P12-7 — No Handshake or Adapter Interaction
FAZA 12 MUST NOT invoke or interact with Handshake, Boundary Adapter,
Interaction Governance Gate, or System Flow.

### P12-8 — No Canon Interaction
FAZA 12 MUST NOT reference, interpret, explain, or depend on Canon content.

### P12-9 — No Behavioral Impact
FAZA 12 MUST NOT alter or influence system behavior, routing, or governance outcomes.

### P12-10 — No Statefulness
FAZA 12 MUST NOT maintain, infer, or depend on state, history, counters, or memory.

### P12-11 — No Persistence
FAZA 12 MUST NOT persist, cache, log, replay, or store any gate-related data.

### P12-12 — No Conditional Control Flow
FAZA 12 MUST NOT introduce branching, retries, fallbacks, loops,
or timing-based logic.

### P12-13 — No Authority Assignment
FAZA 12 MUST NOT assign authority, roles, ownership,
or responsibility related to execution.

### P12-14 — No Visibility Expansion
FAZA 12 MUST NOT expose or leak information about execution capability,
readiness, or internal gate behavior.

### P12-15 — No Extension Surface
FAZA 12 MUST NOT introduce hooks, plugins, configuration switches,
or dynamic decision vocabularies.

### P12-16 — No Testing or Simulation
FAZA 12 MUST NOT include tests, mocks, stubs, emulators, or simulators.

### P12-17 — No Implicit Guarantees
FAZA 12 MUST NOT imply availability, safety, correctness,
or future execution success.

### Binding Clause
Any violation of the above constitutes a Phase 12 breach
and triggers Canon Breach Protocol procedures.
