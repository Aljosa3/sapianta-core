## PHASE 07 — EXPLICIT PROHIBITIONS

### P7-1 — No Execution
FAZA 7 MUST NOT execute any code, logic, or process.

### P7-2 — No Core Invocation
FAZA 7 MUST NOT invoke, call, or trigger any Core logic or functionality.

### P7-3 — No Canon Interpretation
FAZA 7 MUST NOT interpret, explain, reference, or derive meaning from Canon content.

### P7-4 — No Canon Mutation
FAZA 7 MUST NOT modify, extend, annotate, or reframe Canon in any form.

### P7-5 — No Semantic Inference
FAZA 7 MUST NOT infer semantics from handshake responses or status signals.

### P7-6 — No Behavioral Conditioning
FAZA 7 MUST NOT condition Interaction behavior on any Core-originated signal.

### P7-7 — No State Assumptions
FAZA 7 MUST NOT assume Core state, readiness, intent, or availability beyond declared status.

### P7-8 — No Persistence
FAZA 7 MUST NOT persist, cache, replay, or log handshake data for later use.

### P7-9 — No Retry or Escalation
FAZA 7 MUST NOT implement retry logic, fallback paths, or escalation mechanisms.

### P7-10 — No Extension Surface
FAZA 7 MUST NOT introduce extensibility points, hooks, plugins, or override mechanisms.

### P7-11 — No Bidirectional Flow
FAZA 7 MUST NOT enable conversational, streaming, or bidirectional communication.

### P7-12 — No Cross-Layer Leakage
FAZA 7 MUST NOT expose Core details to Interaction or higher layers.

### P7-13 — No Testing or Simulation
FAZA 7 MUST NOT include tests, mocks, simulations, or stubs involving Core.

### P7-14 — No Execution Flags
FAZA 7 MUST NOT define flags, modes, or markers that could later enable execution.

### P7-15 — No Implicit Guarantees
FAZA 7 MUST NOT imply availability, correctness, safety, or future capability.

### Binding Clause
Any violation of the above constitutes a Phase 07 breach and triggers
Canon Breach Protocol procedures.
