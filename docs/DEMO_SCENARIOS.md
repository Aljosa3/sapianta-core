# Demo Scenarios

## Purpose

This document preserves demo continuity across future human and AI assistant sessions. It defines the intended presentation order, narrative flow, and safe talking points for the server/demo branch.

This document is documentation-only. It does not activate runtime governance, enforcement, policy logic, or Decision Spine behavior.

## Enterprise Presentation Order

Recommended enterprise presentation order:

1. Open with SAPIANTA as an AI Decision Validator.
2. Show the cinematic enterprise UI.
3. Present a decision validation scenario.
4. Walk through the audit viewer.
5. Explain the deterministic validation narrative.
6. Show explainability evidence.
7. Connect the flow to EU AI Act-oriented concerns.
8. Show Swagger/API surfaces for technical credibility.
9. Close with limitations and future product direction.

## Cinematic Narrative

The cinematic narrative should communicate that SAPIANTA makes AI-assisted decisions more inspectable.

The first impression should be:

- serious
- enterprise-ready
- audit-oriented
- explainable
- deterministic
- constraint-aware

The cinematic layer should not imply autonomous enforcement, hidden governance activation, or certified compliance.

## Audit Walkthrough Flow

Recommended audit walkthrough:

1. Select or present a sample AI-assisted decision.
2. Show the validation status.
3. Show the supporting explanation.
4. Show the audit record or trace.
5. Identify what the system can display today.
6. Identify what remains outside the current implementation.

The audit walkthrough should prioritize credibility over spectacle.

## EU AI Act Narrative Flow

Recommended EU AI Act narrative:

1. State that the demo does not claim legal compliance or certification.
2. Explain that the product direction is aligned with auditability, explainability, traceability, and human oversight concerns.
3. Show how the audit viewer supports inspection of decision records.
4. Show how deterministic validation can support consistent review.
5. Distinguish implemented demo surfaces from future compliance work.

Safe phrasing:

"SAPIANTA is designed to make AI-assisted decisions more inspectable for regulated environments. This demo illustrates auditability and explainability concepts; it does not certify compliance."

## Swagger/API Demo Flow

Recommended API demo flow:

1. Open Swagger/API documentation after the UI narrative.
2. Show that the product surface has an inspectable technical interface.
3. Demonstrate or describe validation endpoints only as implemented.
4. Use deterministic examples where possible.
5. Avoid suggesting policy engine activation or governed enforcement unless separately implemented.

## Demo Continuity Notes

Future demo sessions should preserve these points:

- The product story is AI Decision Validator.
- The UI is cinematic but enterprise-readable.
- Audit-first presentation is central.
- EU AI Act messaging is narrative support, not a compliance claim.
- Governance remains dormant in the demo branch.
- Runtime governance activation is not active.
- The demo is presentation-oriented and constraint-aware.
