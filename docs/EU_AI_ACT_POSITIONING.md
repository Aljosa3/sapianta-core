# EU AI Act Positioning

## Purpose

This document preserves safe product-facing EU AI Act messaging for the server/demo branch.

It is documentation-only. It does not provide legal advice, claim compliance, certify the product, activate governance, or modify enforcement behavior.

## EU AI Act Narrative

SAPIANTA can be presented as a product direction aligned with enterprise concerns raised by AI regulation: auditability, explainability, traceability, human oversight, and clear limits on system behavior.

The demo should state that it illustrates product concepts relevant to regulated AI conversations. It must not state or imply that the current branch is EU AI Act compliant or certified.

## Compliance Positioning

Safe positioning:

"SAPIANTA is designed to support inspection and explanation of AI-assisted decisions. The current demo illustrates auditability and explainability concepts that may be relevant to regulated AI discussions."

Avoid:

- claiming EU AI Act compliance
- claiming certification
- claiming legal approval
- claiming complete risk classification coverage
- claiming automated regulatory enforcement

## Explainability Positioning

Implemented:

- demo-oriented explanation surfaces can support product walkthroughs
- explanations can help viewers understand validation outcomes

Conceptual:

- explanation flows may support future regulated AI workflows
- role-specific explanations may improve enterprise adoption

Future work:

- richer explanation records
- exportable explanation summaries
- clearer mapping from decision scenario to evidence

## Audit Positioning

Implemented:

- audit-first presentation can show decision records, validation status, and explanation evidence where available in the demo

Conceptual:

- audit views can support regulatory and enterprise conversations
- audit trails can help organizations reason about AI-assisted decisions

Future work:

- stronger audit filtering
- persistent demo evidence exports
- scenario-specific audit walkthroughs

## Deterministic Governance Positioning

Implemented:

- deterministic product messaging and validation presentation should be used where behavior is implemented
- the demo can describe predictable validation as a product principle

Conceptual:

- deterministic governance can be discussed as a future architecture direction
- governance visibility can help explain boundaries and accountability

Future work:

- governance visualization ideas
- clearer implemented-versus-conceptual labels
- expanded deterministic validation scenarios

## Explicit Capability Boundary

The server/demo branch does not currently claim:

- EU AI Act compliance
- legal certification
- active runtime governance
- governed enforcement
- autonomous regulatory execution
- replacement of human legal, compliance, or risk review

All EU AI Act messaging must remain credible, explainable, enterprise-readable, and constraint-aware.
