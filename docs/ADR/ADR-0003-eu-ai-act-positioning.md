# ADR-0003: EU AI Act Positioning

## Status

Accepted for product messaging direction.

## Context

Enterprise buyers may evaluate SAPIANTA through the lens of regulated AI expectations, including auditability, explainability, traceability, and human oversight.

## Decision

Position the demo as relevant to EU AI Act-oriented conversations without claiming compliance, certification, legal approval, or complete regulatory coverage.

## Product Rationale

Careful EU AI Act messaging supports enterprise credibility. It helps explain why auditability and explainability matter while preserving accurate capability boundaries.

## Boundaries

This ADR is not legal advice. It does not certify SAPIANTA and does not activate regulatory enforcement or runtime governance.

## Consequences

All EU AI Act demo language must distinguish implemented, conceptual, and future capabilities.
