# ADR-0002: Audit-First Demo

## Status

Accepted for product demo direction.

## Context

SAPIANTA's clearest current enterprise value is making AI-assisted decision validation inspectable. A demo that leads with general product claims risks overstatement.

## Decision

Use an audit-first demo structure. The product walkthrough should show decision records, validation status, explanation evidence, and limitations early.

## Product Rationale

Audit-first presentation makes the demo credible for compliance, technical, and executive audiences. It anchors the story in visible evidence.

## Boundaries

This is a product presentation decision only. It does not create legal audit readiness, active governed enforcement, or runtime governance activation.

## Consequences

Future demo work should prioritize audit viewer clarity, deterministic example records, and explanation visibility.
