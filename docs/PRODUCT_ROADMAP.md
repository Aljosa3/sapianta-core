# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap preserves product-facing continuity for the server/demo branch. It separates active product work, future product work, and experimental ideas.

It is documentation-only and does not activate runtime governance, enforcement, policy changes, or Decision Spine changes.

## Active Product Work

Current active work:

- enterprise demo flow
- cinematic UI polish
- audit viewer improvements
- AI Decision Validator positioning
- EU AI Act narrative clarity
- explainability flow
- Swagger/API demo exposure
- product memory documentation

## Future Product Work

Future product work may include:

- enterprise onboarding flow
- clearer sample decision packets
- improved audit record filtering
- role-specific demo paths for executive, compliance, and technical audiences
- guided API walkthroughs
- exportable demo evidence summaries
- customer-ready deployment narrative
- stronger separation between implemented, simulated, and conceptual surfaces

Future work must continue to distinguish product presentation from runtime enforcement.

## Experimental Ideas

Experimental ideas may include:

- governance visualization ideas
- timeline-based validation replay
- risk category exploration views
- comparative validation scenarios
- interactive explainability maps
- enterprise readiness scorecards
- domain-specific validator demo packs

Experimental ideas are not implemented capabilities until separately built, tested, and documented.

## Enterprise Onboarding

Enterprise onboarding should explain:

- what the AI Decision Validator is
- what inputs are inspected
- what validation evidence is shown
- what limitations remain
- where human responsibility remains

Onboarding should be short, inspectable, and claim-aware.

## Cinematic UI Polish

Cinematic polish should improve first impression and narrative clarity. It should support, not replace, transparent product explanation.

## Audit Viewer Improvements

Audit viewer improvements should prioritize:

- scan-friendly evidence
- deterministic status display
- explanation visibility
- clear timestamps or scenario markers
- demo-friendly sample records

## Governance Visualization Ideas

Governance visualization ideas are product presentation concepts only unless implemented separately. They should show structure, traceability, and boundaries without suggesting active enforcement.

## Enterprise Demo Flow

The enterprise demo flow should move from product identity to validation, audit, explainability, regulatory narrative, and API exposure.

## AI Decision Validator Positioning

The product should continue to be positioned as a deterministic AI Decision Validator that helps organizations inspect and explain AI-assisted decision records.
