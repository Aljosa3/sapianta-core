# Current Product Focus

## Active Focus

The current product focus for the server/demo branch is a credible enterprise demo of SAPIANTA as an AI Decision Validator.

Priority areas:

- cinematic demo
- audit viewer
- enterprise UI
- AI Decision Validator messaging
- EU AI Act narrative
- demo credibility
- explainability flow

## Cinematic Demo

The demo should create immediate product clarity. The first experience should feel polished and serious, with visible validation, audit, and explanation surfaces.

The cinematic layer exists to help enterprise viewers understand the product quickly. It should not imply hidden runtime control or autonomous governance.

## Audit Viewer

The audit viewer is a central demo surface. It should make validation evidence, status, and explanation records easy to inspect.

The audit viewer should be framed as product-facing transparency, not as proof of legal compliance or active enforcement.

## Enterprise UI

The UI should remain dark, restrained, and enterprise-readable. It should support repeated inspection by technical, compliance, and executive viewers.

The product should feel serious without becoming visually dense or obscure.

## AI Decision Validator Messaging

SAPIANTA should be explained as an AI Decision Validator for inspecting AI-assisted decisions. The strongest current message is deterministic validation with audit-friendly explanation.

The message should avoid claiming autonomous decision-making, legal certification, or active runtime governance.

## EU AI Act Narrative

The EU AI Act narrative should help viewers understand how SAPIANTA can support auditability, explainability, traceability, and risk discussion.

It must not claim implemented compliance, legal approval, certification, or complete regulatory coverage.

## What Is Intentionally Not Being Worked On

The current product focus does not include:

- governance evolution memory
- runtime governance activation
- autonomous governance execution
- enforcement activation
- Decision Spine changes
- policy engine changes
- production compliance certification
- autonomous AGI framing

These exclusions protect demo credibility and prevent accidental overclaiming.

## Why Product Clarity Matters Most

Product clarity currently matters most because the demo branch must be understandable across enterprise, technical, compliance, and investor conversations.

Clear product memory helps future Codex, Claude, GPT, and human sessions keep the same message: SAPIANTA is currently a deterministic, explainable, audit-oriented demo surface, not a hidden enforcement system or certified compliance engine.
