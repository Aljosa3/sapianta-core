# Enterprise Positioning

## Purpose

This document preserves product-facing enterprise positioning for the server/demo branch.

It is documentation-only and does not define runtime behavior, enforcement, governance activation, legal compliance, or certification.

## AI Decision Validator Positioning

SAPIANTA is positioned as an AI Decision Validator for enterprise settings where AI-assisted decisions need to be inspectable, explainable, and audit-friendly.

Current product message:

"SAPIANTA helps organizations inspect AI-assisted decisions through deterministic validation, visible explanations, and audit-oriented presentation."

## Enterprise Trust Narrative

The enterprise trust narrative is based on clarity, limits, and evidence.

SAPIANTA should be presented as a product direction that helps organizations see what happened around an AI-assisted decision, why a validation result was shown, and what record can be reviewed.

Trust should come from transparent constraints, not from broad claims.

## Auditability Narrative

Auditability is central to the current product story. The demo should show how decision records and validation outcomes can be presented for review.

Current product reality:

- audit-oriented UI can present decision validation evidence
- demo scenarios can show records and explanations
- audit views support product storytelling

Non-implemented capability:

- the demo does not prove complete legal audit readiness
- the demo does not activate governed enforcement
- the demo does not certify regulatory compliance

## Explainability Narrative

Explainability should be framed as making validation outputs easier to inspect.

Current product reality:

- explanations can be shown as part of the demo flow
- explanation visibility supports enterprise discussion

Future vision:

- richer explanation trails
- role-specific explanations
- exportable evidence summaries

Non-implemented capability:

- explanations are not legal determinations
- explanations do not replace human review

## Governance Narrative

Governance should be described carefully.

Current product reality:

- governance remains dormant in the server/demo branch
- runtime governance activation is not active
- product surfaces may refer to governance as a visible concept or future direction

Future vision:

- clearer governance visualization
- stronger traceability surfaces
- enterprise controls designed around human accountability

Non-implemented capability:

- no autonomous governance execution
- no governed enforcement
- no hidden activation semantics

## Deterministic Validation Narrative

The deterministic validation narrative should emphasize predictable review behavior.

Current product reality:

- validation messaging should be deterministic and repeatable where implemented
- demo scenarios should avoid probabilistic or autonomous claims

Future vision:

- expanded deterministic validator scenarios
- domain-specific validation examples
- clearer API-linked evidence records

Non-implemented capability:

- deterministic validation does not equal legal compliance
- deterministic validation does not equal complete policy enforcement
