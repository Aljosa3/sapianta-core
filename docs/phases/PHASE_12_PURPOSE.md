## PHASE 12 — EXECUTION GATE (NO EXECUTION)

### Status
- Phase: 12
- State: DESIGN ONLY
- Execution: FORBIDDEN
- Canon: LOCKED
- Canon Breach Protocol: ACTIVE

### Purpose
The sole purpose of Phase 12 is to formally define the existence,
boundaries, and decision vocabulary of an Execution Gate as a
conceptual mechanism, without enabling, permitting, or performing
execution.

### Explicit Scope
Phase 12 defines ONLY:
- The Execution Gate as a formal boundary
- The notion of gate decisions as declarations
- The separation between gate declaration and execution

### Explicit Non-Scope
Phase 12 MUST NOT:
- Execute or simulate execution
- Grant permission or authorization
- Evaluate conditions or inputs
- Trigger or invoke any component
- Interact with Core, Handshake, Adapter, Governance Gate, or Flow
- Interpret Canon or Core behavior

### Authority Boundary
The Execution Gate in Phase 12 is non-operational and non-authoritative.
It declares structure only and carries no execution power.

### Outcome
After Phase 12, execution remains fully prohibited.
The system contains a formally declared gate concept without behavior.
