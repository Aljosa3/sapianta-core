## PHASE 15 — CONTROLLED OUTPUT DECLARATION (NO EXECUTION)

### Status
- Phase: 15
- State: DESIGN ONLY
- Execution: FORBIDDEN
- Canon: LOCKED
- Canon Breach Protocol: ACTIVE

### Purpose
The sole purpose of Phase 15 is to formally declare the concept of
controlled output as a distinct, non-operational notion, enabling
the existence of system output without permitting execution,
authorization, intent formation, or side effects.

### Explicit Scope
Phase 15 defines ONLY:
- Controlled output as a declared concept
- The separation between output and execution
- The separation between output and intent
- The separation between output and authorization
- The requirement that output cannot trigger, imply, or evolve into action

### Explicit Non-Scope
Phase 15 MUST NOT:
- Execute or simulate execution
- Grant permission or authorization
- Declare or validate execution intent
- Trigger or invoke any component
- Interact with Execution Gate, Authorization, Core, Handshake, Adapter, Governance Gate, or System Flow
- Interpret Canon or Core behavior
- Modify system state or environment

### Authority Boundary
Phase 15 is strictly non-authoritative.
It declares output as a concept only and carries no decision power.

### Outcome
After Phase 15, execution remains fully prohibited.
Controlled output exists only as a declared, non-operational concept,
with no capability to influence system behavior.
