## PHASE 14 — EXECUTION INTENT VALIDATION (NO EXECUTION)

### Status
- Phase: 14
- State: DESIGN ONLY
- Execution: FORBIDDEN
- Canon: LOCKED
- Canon Breach Protocol: ACTIVE

### Purpose
The sole purpose of Phase 14 is to formally declare the concept of
execution intent validation as a distinct, non-operational notion,
separate from intent declaration, authorization, and execution.

### Explicit Scope
Phase 14 defines ONLY:
- Validation of execution intent as a declared concept
- The separation between intent validation and execution
- The separation between intent validation and authorization
- The requirement that declared intent cannot be acted upon
  without prior validation in a future phase

### Explicit Non-Scope
Phase 14 MUST NOT:
- Execute or simulate execution
- Grant permission or authorization
- Perform validation logic or decision-making
- Trigger or invoke any component
- Interact with Execution Gate, Authorization, Core, Handshake, Adapter, Governance Gate, or System Flow
- Interpret Canon or Core behavior

### Authority Boundary
Phase 14 is strictly non-authoritative.
It declares validation as a concept only and carries no decision power.

### Outcome
After Phase 14, execution remains fully prohibited.
Execution intent validation exists only as a declared, non-operational concept.
