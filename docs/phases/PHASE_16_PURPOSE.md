## PHASE 16 — CONTROLLED INTERACTION DECLARATION (NO EXECUTION)

### Status
- Phase: 16
- State: DESIGN ONLY
- Execution: FORBIDDEN
- Canon: LOCKED
- Canon Breach Protocol: ACTIVE

### Purpose
The sole purpose of Phase 16 is to formally declare the concept of
controlled interaction as a distinct, non-operational notion,
allowing interaction to exist without enabling execution,
authorization, intent formation, or side effects.

### Explicit Scope
Phase 16 defines ONLY:
- Controlled interaction as a declared concept
- The separation between interaction and execution
- The separation between interaction and intent
- The separation between interaction and authorization
- The requirement that interaction cannot trigger, imply, or evolve into action

### Explicit Non-Scope
Phase 16 MUST NOT:
- Execute or simulate execution
- Grant permission or authorization
- Declare or validate execution intent
- Trigger or invoke any component
- Interact with Execution Gate, Authorization, Core, Handshake, Adapter, Governance Gate, or System Flow
- Interpret Canon or Core behavior
- Modify system state or environment

### Authority Boundary
Phase 16 is strictly non-authoritative.
It declares interaction as a concept only and carries no decision power.

### Outcome
After Phase 16, execution remains fully prohibited.
Controlled interaction exists only as a declared, non-operational concept,
with no capability to influence system behavior.
