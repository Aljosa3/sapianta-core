## PHASE 11 — EXECUTION AUTHORIZATION DECLARATION (NO EXECUTION)

### Status
- Phase: 11
- State: DESIGN ONLY
- Execution: FORBIDDEN
- Canon: LOCKED
- Canon Breach Protocol: ACTIVE

### Purpose
The sole purpose of Phase 11 is to formally declare
the conditions under which execution MAY be authorized
in a future phase, without enabling, permitting,
or implying execution in any form.

### Explicit Scope
Phase 11 defines ONLY:
- The concept of execution authorization as a declaration
- The existence of authorization conditions
- The separation between authorization declaration and execution

### Explicit Non-Scope
Phase 11 MUST NOT:
- Grant permission to execute
- Enable execution
- Trigger execution
- Invoke Core, Handshake, Adapter, or Governance Gate
- Modify system flow
- Interpret Canon or Core behavior

### Authority Boundary
Phase 11 is strictly non-authoritative.
It declares conditions only and carries no decision power.

### Outcome
After Phase 11, execution remains fully prohibited.
Only the concept of future authorization is formally defined.
